# Project Meridian — Billing Data Modernisation

Project Meridian migrates the Atlas Billing Service off the legacy Ledgerline database and onto the Helios Data Platform, so that invoice data is available for analytics in near real time.

- Executive sponsor: Daniel Reyes
- Delivery lead: Kenji Watanabe
- Budget: USD 1.2 million
- Target completion: Q1 2027

## Risks
- Invoice records contain customer payment details and are classified as Restricted, so the migrated data must comply with the residency rules of the platform's governing policy.
- Cut-over requires a billing freeze of up to 6 hours.

## Milestones
1. Dual-write from Atlas to Ledgerline and Helios — November 2026.
2. Reconciliation sign-off by Finance — December 2026.
3. Ledgerline decommissioned — March 2027.
