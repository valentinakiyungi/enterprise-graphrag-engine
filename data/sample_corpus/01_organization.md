# Kestrel Logistics — Organization Overview (FY2026)

Kestrel Logistics is a freight and fulfilment company headquartered in Nairobi with regional hubs in Rotterdam and Singapore.

## Executive Leadership

- **Daniel Reyes** is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO). He is accountable for all engineering teams and the technology roadmap.
- **Lena Fischer** is the Chief Data Officer (CDO). She owns the company data strategy and the analytics organisation.
- **Grace Mwangi** is the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO). She is accountable for information security, risk and compliance.
- **Marcus Bell** is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO). He oversees finance, procurement and all strategic vendor relationships.

## Engineering & Data Teams

### Data Platform Team
The Data Platform Team is led by **Amara Okafor**, who reports to Daniel Reyes. The team builds and operates the company's shared data infrastructure and is on call for platform-level incidents.

### Customer Analytics Team
The Customer Analytics Team is led by **Priya Nair**, who reports to Lena Fischer. The team builds reporting and insight products for the commercial organisation.

### Payments Engineering
Payments Engineering is led by **Kenji Watanabe**, who reports to Daniel Reyes. The team is responsible for invoicing, billing and payment integrations.

### Security Operations
Security Operations is led by **Tomás Herrera**, who reports to Grace Mwangi. The team runs the security operations centre (SOC), access reviews and vulnerability management.

## Procurement
Vendor management is run by **Sofia Lindqvist**, Head of Procurement, who reports to Marcus Bell.
